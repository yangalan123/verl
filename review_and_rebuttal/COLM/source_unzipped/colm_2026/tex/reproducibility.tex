\section*{Reproducibility Statement}
To ensure the reproducibility of our results, we provide comprehensive details of our methodology and experimental setup. \textbf{Algorithm details} for Exploratory Annealed Decoding (EAD), including the annealing schedule and importance sampling correction, are described in \cref{sec: method}. Our \textbf{experimental setup}, including models, datasets, and training frameworks, is detailed in \cref{sec: experiment_setup}. Specific \textbf{hyperparameters} for all experiments are provided in \cref{sec: experiment_setup} and further detailed in \cref{app: minimal_rl_training_details}. We detail our \textbf{evaluation metrics} (e.g., Pass@16, Worst@16) and protocols throughout \cref{sec:experiments}. The relationship between temperature and entropy, central to our motivation, is formally proven in \cref{app:entropy_proof}. All experiments were conducted using publicly available models and datasets, with references provided in \cref{sec: experiment_setup}. We plan to release our code in the formal version to facilitate further research.
